# Product context

## What LiveLayer is
LiveLayer puts a layer of numbers over an event, bar by bar and hour by hour, in kroner. The promise is "Hvert event klogere end det sidste" ("Every event smarter than the last").

## Name
Write **LiveLayer** in text, with two capitals. The domain stays livelayer.dk.

## The problem we name
A festival runs for only a few days a year. That leaves a few hours to test anything, and then a year of waiting for the answer, so most decisions are made on gut feeling.

## Relay (the engine)
Relay is the engine inside LiveLayer. It pulls out what worked, and what it cost, from every event, then uses it when the next event is planned. Every festival is described in the same festival model (bars, zones, programme, crowd, hours), so a lesson from one festival can be adapted to another festival's context. Relay shares lessons, not numbers: other festivals never see your sales. "Persondata slettes. Læringen bliver." Relay learns from two sources: the festival's own seasons, year over year, and other festivals modelled into the festival's own context. Many factors are in play (ticket sales, programme, weather, site, the other vendors), so recommendations come as ranges from data, with the management's own decision margin (e.g. 12.5 %) on top. Organisations that run many events learn faster, because every event becomes a test.

## Offers shown on the site (only these)
1. **Indsigt / Insight**: what happened at the last event, and what did it cost? It shows where queues cost sales and where capacity stood empty, for every bar and every hour. (Previously called "Sidste år i kroner".)
2. **Plan / Plan**: what to do differently next time. Each concrete recommendation carries a value in kroner, a reason and a confidence level; the festival decides. The case shown has 11 recommendations worth +420,000 kr. (Previously called "Næste års beslutninger".)

## What the offers enable
- Volunteers placed where the crowd is.
- Up-front estimates for vendors.
- Payment terminals where the queues are.
- A budget built on numbers.
- Less uncertainty, plus time and money saved for organisers and vendors.
